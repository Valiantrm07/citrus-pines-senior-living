Personalized Wellness Matrix

Care Plans That Evolve With Your Needs

At Citrus Pines, we understand that needs change over time. Our progressive care level system allows residents to stay in their beloved community while receiving seamless adjustments to their support plan.

Level 1: Independent Support

Minimal assistance, focused on lifestyle convenience, dining, and social events.

Level 2: Moderate Assistance

Help with daily dressing, medication management, and mobility guidance.

Level 3: High Care & Memory Specialization

Comprehensive caregiver support, full daily living help, and secure memory engagement.
